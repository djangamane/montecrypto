Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2024

MonteCrypto ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with the AI Crypto Risk Assessment platform, subscribe to the Weekly Risk Brief, or purchase premium services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and encrypted password provided during sign-up.
  • Subscription data: billing details handled by PayPal and Coinbase Commerce.
  • Usage data: anonymized analytics such as page views, feature usage, and device metadata.
  • Support messages: communications sent to our support team.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and secure access to dashboards and newsletters.
  • Deliver transactional emails, product updates, and the Weekly Risk Brief.
  • Monitor performance, prevent abuse, and improve the quality of risk assessments.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to regulatory requests when required.

3. Data processors

We rely on reputable processors for hosting, analytics, payments, and email delivery. These include Supabase, Vercel, PayPal, Coinbase Commerce, and Resend. Each processor is bound by contractual safeguards and only processes data as needed to provide their service to us.

4. Cookies and tracking

The site uses essential cookies to maintain sessions. Analytics events are aggregated and do not store personally identifiable information. You can opt out of analytics via browser settings or add-ons.

5. Data retention

Account and subscription records are retained while your membership is active and for up to 24 months after termination for compliance. Support communications are stored for 12 months. You may request deletion sooner by contacting privacy@montecrypto.ai.

6. Security

We implement access controls, encryption in transit, and logging to protect your data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so maintain unique passwords and monitor account activity.

7. Your rights

  • Request a copy of personal data we store about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally permissible.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

8. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our providers operate. We ensure adequate protections consistent with applicable laws.

9. Updates

We may revise this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be announced via email or product notifications. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, email privacy@montecrypto.ai.
