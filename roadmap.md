🎯 The Smart Order - Why This Way?
Phase 1: Core Mechanics First (Weeks 1-2)
Rationale: Make the game FUN before anything else. If it's not engaging, nothing else matters. This includes:

Question bank expansion (you'll provide these)
Difficulty scaling (harder levels keep players engaged)
Power-ups (freeze gun, ammo boosts—game-changers!)
CEX/DEX integration (complete the learning journey)
Why now? These are the foundation. If we add paywall/auth first and then change core mechanics, we risk breaking things users already paid for.

Phase 2: Visual Polish (Week 3)
Rationale: Once gameplay is solid, make it gorgeous. Background images and video lessons don't affect game logic—low risk of bugs.

Why now? People will tolerate bugs in beta, but not ugly visuals in a paid product.

Phase 3: Engagement Features (Week 4)
Rationale: Streaks and Hall of Fame are "nice-to-haves" that increase retention but aren't core to gameplay.

Why now? After the game is fun and pretty, add the dopamine hooks.

Phase 4: Monetization (Week 5)
Rationale: Don't gate incomplete content. Wait until everything is polished.

Why last? If we add paywall early and then break something in the game, we have angry paying customers.

Phase 5: Integration (Week 6)
Rationale: Clean integration when everything else is stable.
