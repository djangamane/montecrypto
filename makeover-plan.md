# Website Makeover Plan

This document outlines the plan for a "mini makeover" of the website to improve its content, credibility, and user experience, moving it towards a stronger MVP.

## 1. Branding and Content Focus

**Goal:** Sharpen the website's focus on the AI crypto tool and newsletter.

*   **Action:** Systematically remove all references to "MonteCrypto" from the codebase.
*   **Action:** Refine the website's copy to clearly communicate the value proposition of the AI tool and the newsletter.

## 2. Content Depth and Clarity

**Goal:** Explain what the product does and why it's valuable.

*   **Action:** Create a new section explaining "How It Works", detailing the AI-driven analysis process.
*   **Action:** Enhance the hero section with a clear, concise explanation of the product.

## 3. Trust and Credibility

**Goal:** Build user trust by showcasing expertise and providing transparency.

*   **Action:** Create a photo carousel in the "About Me" section to visually represent your experience.
*   **Action:** Add your Bitcoin Certified Professional Certificate to the "About Me" section.
*   **Action:** Add a section for sample reports or screenshots of the tool in action (using dummy data if necessary).
*   **Action:** (Future) Add a section for client testimonials or pilot results as they become available.

## 4. Visuals and Engagement

**Goal:** Make the site more visually appealing and engaging.

*   **Action:** Research and implement a lightweight solution for adding animated spinning Bitcoins or similar effects at key points on the page.
*   **Action:** (Optional) Replace the static background with a video background. We need to ensure this doesn't negatively impact performance.
*   **Action:** Create and add icons to represent key features of the tool.
*   **Action:** Design and add charts or infographics to visualize risk metrics in the sample reports.

## 5. User Experience (UX) and Call-to-Action (CTA)

**Goal:** Guide users towards key actions and ensure a smooth experience.

*   **Action:** Add a prominent CTA button in the hero section (e.g., "Get Your Risk Score", "Try Now").
*   **Action:** Strategically place additional CTAs throughout the page.
*   **Action:** Thoroughly test the website on various mobile and tablet devices to ensure a responsive and user-friendly experience.

## 6. SEO and Discovery

**Goal:** Improve the website's visibility in search engines.

*   **Action:** Review and optimize all page titles, meta descriptions, and headings for relevant keywords.

## 7. Legal and Compliance

**Goal:** Protect the business and inform users.

*   **Action:** Add a clear disclaimer stating that the tool is for informational purposes and not financial advice.
*   **Action:** Ensure a Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are in place and easily accessible. (I see `public/legal` folder, so these might already exist, but we should check them).

## 8. Performance

**Goal:** Maintain a fast and responsive website.

*   **Action:** As we add new visual elements (images, videos, animations), we will monitor the site's performance using tools like Lighthouse and PageSpeed Insights.
*   **Action:** We will implement performance best practices, such as image optimization and lazy loading.
